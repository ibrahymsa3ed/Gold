## Full Description (≤4000 chars)

### English

**InstaGold** is the fastest way to check live gold prices in Egypt.

**Live Prices**
- 21 Karat, 24 Karat, 18 Karat, 14 Karat
- International Gold Ounce (USD)
- Egyptian Gold Pound (EGP)
- Live USD/EGP exchange rate

**Prices from Egypt's leading gold shops**: SAM, L'AZURDE, BTC, SHEHATA — compare buy and sell prices side by side.

**My Gold Portfolio**
Add your gold pieces with weight, karat, and purchase price. The app calculates your total holding value in real time against current market prices.

**Savings & Goals**
Set a savings goal for a gold purchase. Track your progress. The calculator shows you the exact cost including tax/hallmark fees and manufacturing charges — so you know exactly what you'll pay at the jeweller.

**Gold Calculator**
Enter weight and karat → get the expected price with and without manufacturing + tax (10 EGP/gram default, editable).

**Smart Notifications**
Get notified at key times (07:00, 11:00, 15:00, 19:00 Cairo time) when prices move significantly. Price-change alerts let you act at the right moment.

**Home Screen Widget**
Add the widget to your home screen and see 21K, 24K, and Ounce sell prices at a glance — no need to open the app.

**Fully Arabic**
Complete Arabic UI with RTL layout, Arabic numerals, and natural Arabic phrasing.
